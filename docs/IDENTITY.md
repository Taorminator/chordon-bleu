# Brand identity — CHORDON BLEU

Official name: **CHORDON BLEU**. GitHub slug: `chordon-bleu`. Decorative wordmark may use **CHORD—ON—BLEU**.

Manufacturer signature: **Middle Age Engineering**, abbreviated **MID Engineering**. A playful homage, not a Teenage Engineering affiliation.

Tagline: *Cook up something beautiful.*

Base color midnight `#0d1020` with neon `#4657FF`, passing through violet `#a66ae3`, pink `#d66aa2`, coral `#fa8078` and warm amber `#ffad53`. Temperature is an expressive metaphor, never a scientifically objective classification of chord warmth.

Main actions PLAY, FLIP, SAVE; explore controls RECIPES, FEEL, SPICE. Recipe labels are vivid and playful but should remain descriptive and accessible.

All essential controls remain understandable without music theory or the ability to distinguish colors. One musical world per recipe, one-tap selection, no modal multi-step settings.

GHIBLI DREAM is an **unofficial** harmonic-world study inspired by published analyses of Joe Hisaishi's music. It does not provide copyrighted themes, recordings, scores, or approved Ghibli branding.
