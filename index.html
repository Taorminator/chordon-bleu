Quick start.

Tap PLAY. Tap a chord to hear it. FLIP discovers another idea.

  • HARMONY · Choose a key and recipe. SPICE controls variation, FEEL gives it character.
  • CHANGE · KEEP 1 protects your selected chord. ALL 4 reshapes the entire progression.
  • PERFORM · HOLD sustains, PULSE repeats full chords, ARP plays their notes. BPM sets the pace.
  • SEASON · Hold MELT, CHORUS or DELAY to add live effects.
  • PICK / SHAPE · Preview a chord, then set ROOT and TYPE. AUTO keeps discovery open.
  • REC / SAVE · Record four chords, or save a favorite loop to return to.
